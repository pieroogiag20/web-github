El reino del té

Inglaterra es una nación constituyente del Reino Unido cuya capital es Londres y cuya moneda oficial es la libra esterlina. Ocupa 130 395 km² en Gran Bretaña, limita con Escocia y Gales, y concentra cerca del 84 % de la población y el 85 % del PIB del Reino Unido

Himno de Inglaterra

God save our gracious King
Long live our noble King,
God save the King!
Send him victorious,
Happy and glorious,
Long to reign over us,
God save the King!
O Lord our God arise,
Scatter his enemies,
And make them fall!
Confound their politics,
Frustrate their knavish tricks,
On Thee our hopes we fix,
God save us all!
Thy choicest gifts in store,
On him be pleased to pour,
Long may he reign!
May he defend our laws,
And ever give us cause,
To sing with heart and voice,
God save the King!
